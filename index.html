About Kelly House

Established in [To be added], Kelly House provides a safe, nurturing environment for children who have lost their parents or cannot live with their families. Our dedicated staff ensures each child receives personalized care, quality education, and emotional support.

We currently care for 45 children ranging from infants to teenagers, providing them with everything they need to grow into confident, capable adults.
